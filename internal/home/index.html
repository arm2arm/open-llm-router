WHY OPEN LLM ROUTER

Local models. Shared with confidence.

Give your team a consistent way to use your models, with the controls to manage a shared service.

⤴

Bring your existing tools

OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible endpoints support familiar clients, model discovery and streaming responses.

⌘

Access that fits your team

Single sign-on through Keycloak, personal API keys, and group policies that determine which models each user can access.

◷

Keep usage in view

See token usage and daily or monthly quotas. Per-user rate and concurrency limits help share capacity fairly.

⇄

Route across replicas

Load-aware balancing, health checks and failover direct requests across your configured model replicas.
